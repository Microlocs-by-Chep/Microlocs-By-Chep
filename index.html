Our approach starts with the right foundation. Microlocs are installed as twist locs and maintained through interlocking, giving your journey a clear structure from day one.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

How are your microlocs started?

Microlocs by Chep starts microlocs as twist locs, then maintains them through interlocking.

How often should I book a retie?

Your maintenance rhythm depends on your hair and growth. We will recommend a schedule that works for your loc journey.

Can I come if my microlocs were installed elsewhere?

Yes — book an assessment/consultation first so we can understand the condition and structure of your existing locs.

Do you offer microloc extensions?

Yes. Choose Microloc Extensions when booking so we can discuss the length, blend and installation approach you want.

Can I book a house call?

Ask us about current house-call availability when submitting your booking request.
