THE CHEP APPROACH

Tiny locs, built with intention.

Microlocs by Chep is a microlocs-focused brand built around helping clients begin and maintain a beautiful natural-hair loc journey.

Microlocs are smaller or tiny locs that are installed differently. Here, the journey starts with twist locs and is maintained through interlocking — with the foundation, neatness and long-term journey kept at the centre.

Whether you are starting from natural hair, coming in for a retie, or considering extensions for extra length, we can help you choose the right next step.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

What are microlocs?

Microlocs are smaller or tiny locs installed using a method suited to creating a fine, versatile loc structure.

How are Microlocs by Chep locs started?

Microlocs by Chep starts them as twist locs, then maintains them through interlocking.

How often should I book a retie?

Your maintenance rhythm depends on your hair and growth. We will recommend a schedule that works for your loc journey.

Can I come if my microlocs were installed elsewhere?

Yes — book an assessment first so we can understand the condition and structure of your existing locs.

Can I start my microlocs with extensions?

Yes. Select “Fresh Microlocs Installation with Extensions” when booking so we can discuss your desired length, blend and installation plan.

Can I book a house call?

Ask us about current house-call availability when submitting your booking request.
