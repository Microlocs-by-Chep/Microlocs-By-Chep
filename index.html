FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you begin your loc journey.

What are microlocs?

Microlocs are very small, individual locs created with twists, braids or interlocking. Their small size makes them lightweight and versatile for styling.

What is the difference between microlocs and Sisterlocks?

Sisterlocks® is a trademarked hair-locking brand and structured system. The brand sets specific requirements for its training, grid and sizing approach, locking technique, rotations and proprietary tools, with the service normally performed by a practitioner trained in that system.

Microlocs is the broader term used for very small locs that are created or maintained outside the Sisterlocks® branded system. Microlocs can be installed with different parting patterns, sizes, tools and techniques—including twists, braids or interlocking—depending on the client’s hair and the loctician’s method.

The finished styles can look similar, which is why the names are sometimes confused. The clearest difference is not simply the size of the locs: Sisterlocks® refers to a specific branded system, while microlocs describes a wider category of small locs. During consultation, we explain the method we use so each client knows exactly what service they are receiving.

Can microlocs be undone?

Yes, they can sometimes be combed out, especially when they are newer. Removal can take many hours and may cause shedding or breakage, so a professional assessment is recommended.

How often do I need a retie?

Most clients return every 4–8 weeks depending on hair growth, loc size and the condition of the roots.

How long does an installation take?

A fresh installation takes approximately 6–8 hours, depending on hair length and volume.

How is installation pricing calculated?

Pricing is based on hair length and volume. Extensions and colouring are optional add-ons with their own estimates.

How do I choose the best microlocs salon in Nairobi?

The best fit depends on your hair, preferred loc size, budget and maintenance needs. Compare examples of the loctician’s work, ask which installation method they use, and confirm installation and retie costs before booking.
