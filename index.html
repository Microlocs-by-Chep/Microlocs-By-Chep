MICROLOC CARE GUIDE

Understanding the stages of your microloc journey.

There are many beautiful ways to begin, maintain and wear microlocs. The way they look on installation day is only the starting point—your locs will shrink, swell, settle and mature as your hair finds its natural locking pattern.

One question we hear often at Microlocs by Chep is, “What stage am I in?” Every head of hair develops differently, but these general signs can help you understand what may be happening and what to expect next.

A helpful guide, not a strict timetable: These stages mainly describe interlocked microlocs. Hair texture, density, porosity, routine and lifestyle can all affect how quickly your locs progress.

0–3 months

The sprouting stage

Your hair may still resemble small twists, braids or defined curls. The strands are beginning to shrink into their locking pattern, so some frizz, itching, slippage or unraveling can be normal. This is a delicate stage—keep manipulation low and follow the care routine recommended by your loctician.

3–6 months

The baby stage

Shrinkage and volume often increase as the locs begin to swell. Different areas may lock at different speeds because one head can contain several textures. This is not an “ugly stage”; it is an adjustment stage that asks for patience, gentle care and confidence in the process.

6–12 months

The adolescent stage

Your locs may feel more established, although they may not yet be fully mature. Budding can appear as small rounded areas or changes in texture. Some locs begin to drop and gain movement, while light frizz may continue as the internal structure develops.

1–2 years

The teen stage

Many hair textures are substantially locked by now. The locs often look more uniform, reties may become easier, and everyday styling begins to feel more familiar. Some shrinkage can remain, but you may notice greater length, confidence and versatility.

2–5 years

The adult stage

Your length becomes easier to see as shrinkage settles. Slippage is less common, the foundation feels secure, and more styling options become available. This is where many clients begin to experience the freedom and low-maintenance rhythm they hoped for.

5+ years

The mature stage

The loc pattern is well established and consistent. Older ends may need extra attention because they have experienced years of washing, styling and daily wear. A healthy foundation, regular reties and mindful handling help mature microlocs remain strong and beautiful.

Your timeline is personal.

There is no need to compare your progress with someone else’s. We assess your roots, texture, density and overall loc health at every appointment, then guide you according to what your own hair needs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you begin your loc journey.

What are microlocs?

Microlocs are very small, individual locs created with twists, braids or interlocking. Their small size makes them lightweight and versatile for styling.

What is the difference between microlocs and Sisterlocks?

Sisterlocks® is a trademarked hair-locking brand and structured system. The brand sets specific requirements for its training, grid and sizing approach, locking technique, rotations and proprietary tools, with the service normally performed by a practitioner trained in that system.

Microlocs is the broader term used for very small locs that are created or maintained outside the Sisterlocks® branded system. Microlocs can be installed with different parting patterns, sizes, tools and techniques—including twists, braids or interlocking—depending on the client’s hair and the loctician’s method.

The finished styles can look similar, which is why the names are sometimes confused. The clearest difference is not simply the size of the locs: Sisterlocks® refers to a specific branded system, while microlocs describes a wider category of small locs. During consultation, we explain the method we use so each client knows exactly what service they are receiving.

Can microlocs be undone?

Yes, they can sometimes be combed out, especially when they are newer. Removal can take many hours and may cause shedding or breakage, so a professional assessment is recommended.

How often do I need a retie?

Most clients return every 4–8 weeks depending on hair growth, loc size and the condition of the roots.

How long does an installation take?

A fresh installation takes approximately 6–8 hours, depending on hair length and volume.

How is installation pricing calculated?

Pricing is based on hair length and volume. Extensions and colouring are optional add-ons with their own estimates.

How do I choose the best microlocs salon in Nairobi?

The best fit depends on your hair, preferred loc size, budget and maintenance needs. Compare examples of the loctician’s work, ask which installation method they use, and confirm installation and retie costs before booking.
