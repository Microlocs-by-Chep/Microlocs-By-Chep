Microlocs vs Sisterlocks: what is the difference?

Microlocs and Sisterlocks can look similar, but the terms describe different things. If you are comparing microlocs and Sisterlocks in Nairobi, ask which method the practitioner actually offers before booking.

What are microlocs?

Microlocs is a broad term for very small individual locs. They can be created with twists, braids or interlocking, using different parting patterns, tools and techniques according to the hair and the loctician’s method.

What are Sisterlocks?

Sisterlocks® is a trademarked hair-locking brand and structured system with specific requirements for training, grid, sizing, locking technique, rotations and proprietary tools. The name does not simply mean very small locs.

Which service does Microlocs by Chep offer?

Our listed fresh installation service is twist-started microlocs. We also offer interlocking reties and maintenance. This page does not claim Sisterlocks® certification or an official branded Sisterlocks installation service. If you already have Sisterlocks, discuss your existing method and maintenance requirements with the team before booking a retie.

What should you ask before choosing a method?

  • Which installation method and parting pattern will be used?
  • What training does the practitioner have for that method?
  • How much will installation and ongoing maintenance cost?
  • How often should your own hair be retied?
  • How will extensions, colour or future styling affect the plan?

Choose after an assessment of your hair, lifestyle and maintenance needs. No one method is automatically the right choice for every client.
