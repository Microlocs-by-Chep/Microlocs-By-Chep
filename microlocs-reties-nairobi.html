Microlocs reties and maintenance in Nairobi

Microlocs by Chep offers interlocking reties and maintenance at Kipro Centre in Westlands, Nairobi. Reties keep new growth tidy while giving your loctician an opportunity to assess the roots and your loc journey.

How often should microlocs be retied?

Most clients return every 4–8 weeks. The right interval depends on hair growth, loc size and the condition of the roots. Follow your loctician’s guidance rather than a fixed timetable for everyone.

How long is a retie appointment?

Our booking system allows a focused 3-hour appointment for a retie. If you have overdue maintenance or specific concerns, discuss these with the team before choosing your appointment.

Can I book maintenance for existing Sisterlocks?

Tell the team how your locs were installed and maintained so they can assess the service you need. Sisterlocks® is a specific branded system. A microloc interlocking retie should not be assumed to be a certified Sisterlocks service.

How much does a retie cost?

Contact the studio for a current estimate based on your hair and maintenance needs. Confirm any colouring add-on and the total before your appointment.
