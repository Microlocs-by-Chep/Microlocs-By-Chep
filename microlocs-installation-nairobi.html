Microlocs installation in Westlands, Nairobi

Start your microloc journey at Microlocs by Chep in Westlands, Nairobi. We offer twist-started microlocs with careful sizing, parting and aftercare guidance, tailored to your hair and preferred look.

What happens at a microlocs consultation?

We assess your hair length, density and condition, discuss your preferred loc size and volume, and explain the installation method before you book. Ask about extensions if you want added length, and confirm the final estimate with the team.

How much does microlocs installation cost in Nairobi?

Our listed estimates are KSh 10,000–15,000 for short hair with light volume and KSh 15,000–20,000 for long hair with full volume. Your estimate depends on hair length and volume. Extensions and colouring are optional add-ons; confirm your total before your appointment.

How long does installation take?

A fresh installation usually takes approximately 6–8 hours, depending on hair length and volume. A consultation helps us plan the time needed for your hair.

Planning maintenance after installation

Most clients return for a retie every 4–8 weeks, depending on hair growth, loc size and root condition. Follow the aftercare advice given for your own hair.

What should I confirm before my first appointment?

  • Share your current hair length and volume, and any existing locs or extensions.
  • Discuss the loc size and look you want, using reference photos if helpful.
  • Ask the team how to prepare your hair before arrival.
  • Confirm the installation estimate, any add-ons and the time reserved for you.
  • Plan your first maintenance visit and ask for aftercare guidance for your own hair.

Choosing the best microlocs salon in Nairobi for your hair

A useful comparison starts with your hair and the service you need. Before choosing a loctician, check these points rather than relying on a “best salon” label alone.

  • Results: ask to see fresh installations and maintained locs, including examples close to your hair length, volume and preferred size. View our client results and @microlocsbychep.
  • Method: ask how the locs will be started and maintained. Our listed installation is twist-started microlocs; Sisterlocks is a distinct branded system.
  • Total cost: confirm installation, extensions or colouring, and future reties. Use the studio estimates above as a starting point for your consultation.
  • Maintenance: ask for a retie interval suited to your hair, plus aftercare guidance. See our retie appointment guide.
  • Location and availability: choose a studio you can return to for maintenance. We are at Kipro Centre, 2nd Floor, Shop B6, Westlands, Nairobi.

To discuss whether our approach suits you, ask us about your hair and maintenance plan.
